A school day at a glance.

Time at a glance

Large digits, a countdown and segmented progress. The second break is marked as lunchtime.

Fits your screen

Desktop, iPhone or iPad. Light and dark appearance, presentation mode and compact native windows.

Offline, too

Native apps include the timetable and calendar. Online PTB synchronization is optional to operation; offline the clock keeps running.

Built for BBS Einbeck.

Fixed timetable, Europe/Berlin time and Lower Saxony holidays. Calendar coverage: 2026 through 21 August 2030, without automatic updates. School-specific exceptions may differ.

The application UI is German. No account, no telemetry. PTB time synchronization uses an external connection.
